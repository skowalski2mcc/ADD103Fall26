Cereal Singles

Love is part of a balanced breakfast.

Tony the Tiger

Occupation: Professional hype man

Looking For: "Someone who can match my energy. This is probably harder than it sounds."

Green Flag: Will support literally anything you do.

Red Flag: Cannot use an indoor voice.

Ideal Date: Going to the gym and then completely ruining it by getting pancakes afterward.

Personality: They're not just good, they're GR-R-REAT! Fun fact! Tony has been saying this since 1952!

Lucky the Leprechaun

Occupation: Full-time cereal security

Looking For: "Someone adventurous who won't constantly ask where I keep the Lucky Charms."

Green Flag: Always has marshmallows.

Red Flag: Has serious trust issues around children.

Ideal Date: Following a rainbow and seeing where we end up.

Count Chocula

Occupation: Vampire, "technically"

Looking For: "Someone who likes chocolate and doesn't have plans before noon.""

Green Flag: Has a castle.

Red Flag: Has a castle.

Ideal Date: Horror movie marathon at midnight with an unreasonable amount of chocolate.

Cap'n Crunch

Occupation: Captain Of what exactly is unclear

Looking For: "Someone who likes the ocean and doesn't ask too many questions about my qualifications."

Green Flag: Owns a boat.

Red Flag: The implication.

Ideal Date: Sailing somewhere nice and hoping the roof of your mouth survives breakfast.

Buzz the Honey Bee

Occupation: Honey Nut Cheerios spokesperson

Looking For: "Someone sweet who doesn't mind that my entire personality revolves around honey."

Green Flag: Hard worker.

Red Flag: Will bring the entire family See: Hive to your house.

Ideal Date: Walking through a flower garden and trying not to make it weird.

A Few Dating Safety Tips

Always meet for the first time in a public cereal aisle.

Never tell someone where you keep your marshmallows on the first date.

If your date says he is a captain, maybe ask to see some credentials.
