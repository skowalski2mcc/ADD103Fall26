Breakfast Favorites

Three breakfast foods that are worth actually waking up for.

Pancakes

Fluffy, covered in syrup, and almost impossible to be unhappy while eating.

Best with: Butter, maple syrup, and fresh berries.

Omelet

The responsible breakfast choice that still lets you throw cheese into everything.

Best with: Cheese, peppers, onions, and way too much hot sauce.

French Toast

Someone looked at regular toast and decided it simply was not trying hard enough.

Best with: Powdered sugar, strawberries, and maple syrup.
