Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri

Movie Details

Director: Martin McDonagh

Watched: September 19, 2026

My Rating: 4.5/5 stars

My Review

Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri has a really heart-wrenching story, but what stood out to me most was the characters. They're complicated, and the performances really make the whole movie work.

It's definitely an emotional movie, and there were a lot of moments that stuck with me. The story and the memorable performances made this an easy 4.5 stars for me.
