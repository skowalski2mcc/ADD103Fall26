Resident Evil

Movie Details

Director: Zach Cregger

Watched: October 2, 2026

My Rating: 4.5/5 stars

My Review

I don't usually like horror movies, and video game movies aren't normally my thing either, so I was surprised by how much I liked Resident Evil. I thought the director did an excellent job, and I had a really good time watching it.

It definitely wasn't what I expected going in, but in a good way. For a movie that's outside of what I'd normally choose to watch, giving it 4.5 stars says a lot about how much I enjoyed it.
